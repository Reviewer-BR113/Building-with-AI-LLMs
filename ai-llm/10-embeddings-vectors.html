Embeddings & vector search

How does AI find things by meaning? It turns text into numbers — embeddings — arranged so that similar meanings sit close together. Search then becomes "find the nearest points." This is the engine under semantic search and RAG.

01 · The Idea

Turn meaning into a location

Computers compare numbers easily, not meanings. So AI converts each piece of text into a list of numbers positioned in space such that closeness = similarity of meaning. Once meaning is a location, "what's similar?" becomes "what's nearby?" — a problem computers solve fast.

02 · What Is an Embedding

Text in, a list of numbers out

An embedding model reads text and outputs a fixed list of numbers (often hundreds) — its vector. Each number captures some aspect of meaning. Crucially, texts that mean similar things get similar numbers.

03 · Vectors as Points in Space

Similar meanings cluster together

If we imagine those numbers as coordinates, every word becomes a point. Words about the same thing land near each other, forming clusters. (Real embeddings have hundreds of dimensions; here's a 2-D sketch.)

04 · Nearest-Neighbor Search

To search, find the closest points

Now searching is easy: turn the query into a point too, then find the points nearest to it. Those are the most similar in meaning — no matter what exact words they use.

▶ SEARCH BY NEARNESS

05 · Where It's Used

One idea, many features

Embeddings quietly power a huge amount of modern AI. Once you can measure "how similar are these two things by meaning," a lot becomes possible.

🔎 Semantic search

Find results by meaning, not keywords (Lesson 04's AI search).

📚 RAG

Retrieve the most relevant pieces of your documents to answer questions (next lesson).

🎯 Recommendations

"More like this" — items whose embeddings are near what you liked.

🧹 Grouping & dedup

Cluster similar items, or spot near-duplicates, by nearness.

06 · Keep in Mind

The mental model

📍 Meaning = position

Embeddings place similar meanings near each other in space.

📐 Similarity = distance

Closer points mean more similar meaning; that's all "vector search" measures.

🗄️ Stored in a vector DB

A vector database stores embeddings and finds nearest neighbors fast, at scale.
