Real-world limitations

AI is powerful but not reliable. In production it will confidently make things up, drift, and sometimes fail. Shipping safely means building safety nets around it — not trusting it blindly.

01 · The Idea

Treat AI like a brilliant, unreliable intern

It's fast, capable, and sometimes confidently wrong. You wouldn't let an intern email customers or change the database unchecked — same with AI. The whole job of "production" is wrapping the model in checks so its mistakes can't cause harm.

02 · Hallucination in Production

Fluent does not mean correct

A hallucination is when the model states something false as if it were fact — invented citations, fake numbers, made-up features. It happens because the model predicts plausible text, and plausible isn't the same as true.

▶ SPOT THE HALLUCINATION

03 · Validate Outputs Before Use

Put a gate between output and action

Never wire the model's raw output straight into something important. Pass it through a validation gate first: does it have the right shape? Are the values sensible? Only if it passes do you use it.

▶ THROUGH THE GATE

04 · Constraints & Guardrails

Limit what the AI is allowed to say or do

Guardrails are rules that constrain the output: allowed topics, allowed values, forbidden content, safe defaults. They catch bad output before it reaches the user, replacing it with something safe.

▶ GUARDRAILS AT WORK

05 · Fallback Strategies

Always have a plan B

The AI will sometimes fail — a timeout, a bad answer, low confidence. A good feature degrades gracefully: try again, fall back to a simpler method or a cached/default result, and if all else fails, hand off to a human. Never a dead end.

▶ CASCADE THROUGH FALLBACKS

06 · Keep in Mind

The production mindset

🤥 Assume it can be wrong

Design as if any answer might be a hallucination, especially facts and numbers.

🚪 Gate the important stuff

Validate before anything the output touches money, data, or users.

🪂 Fail gracefully

Retry, fall back, or escalate — never leave the user at a dead end.
