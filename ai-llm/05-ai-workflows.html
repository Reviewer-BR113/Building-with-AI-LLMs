Lesson 05 · 🔗 Building with AI

Automating workflows

A single prompt answers a question. But real work is many steps. "Agentic thinking" means letting AI drive a multi-step job — breaking it down, chaining the pieces, and using tools when it needs them.

01 · The Idea

From answering to doing

So far the AI has answered. Automation is about the AI doing — completing a whole task made of several steps, sometimes reaching out to tools (a calculator, a search, a calendar) along the way. This shift from "chatbot" to "worker" is what people mean by agentic.

02 · Break a Task Into Steps

A big job is many small ones

The first move in automation is decomposition: turn one fuzzy goal into an ordered list of concrete steps. Each step is something an AI (or a tool) can reliably do.

▶ DECOMPOSE A GOAL — "publish a weekly newsletter"

03 · Chain Prompts Across Steps

Each step feeds the next

Once you have steps, you chain them: the output of one becomes the input to the next. The app runs them in order automatically — no human copy-pasting between prompts.

▶ RUN THE CHAIN — data flows down

04 · Agent-Like Systems (AI + Tools)

When AI decides to use a tool

LLMs are unreliable at some things — exact math, live data, real actions. The breakthrough: the AI can recognize when it needs help and call a tool, then use the tool's result to answer. It acts, instead of guessing.

▶ WATCH AI REACH FOR A TOOL

05 · Tool Usage — the "Skills" Idea

Give it a toolbox; it picks the tool

You equip the AI with a set of tools — think of them as skills. For each task, the AI chooses the right one. More tools = more it can do. Watch it match tasks to skills:

▶ RIGHT TOOL FOR THE JOB

06 · Keep in Mind

Automation needs a leash

🪜 Small steps win

Break jobs down; each reliable step makes the whole chain reliable.

🧯 Failures compound

If step 2 is wrong, everything after inherits the error. Check between steps.

👀 Keep oversight

An AI taking actions can do real damage — add limits and human checks for risky steps.
